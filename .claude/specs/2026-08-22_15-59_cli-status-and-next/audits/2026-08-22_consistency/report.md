## Executive summary

9 findings total: 1 CRITICAL, 2 HIGH, 4 MEDIUM, 2 INFO.

The most serious issue is a direct contradiction between `_index.md` and `shared-cli.md` on where `@wadeck/shared-cli` source lives (own repo vs. agent-fleet monorepo). D-17 is also duplicated in `wdrive-migration.md` with two different meanings under the same ID. The dependency chain references undefined task IDs (T-NEW, T6). Several smaller inconsistencies affect dependency lists, open-question status, and terminology.
