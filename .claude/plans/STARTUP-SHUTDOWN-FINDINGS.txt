   Location: C:\Workspace_Tooling\agent-fleet\packages\web-backend\src\server.ts:397

   - expirationTimers Map (line 58) never cleared
   - clients Map (line 55) never explicitly closed
   - socket.close() only called on disconnection, not on shutdown

   3. Clear internal state maps
